What does a QA engineer do?

A QA (quality assurance) engineer makes sure software works as intended before it reaches real users. Typically they:

  • turn requirements into clear acceptance criteria and test cases;
  • test by hand and with automation, and re-test after every change;
  • find, report and follow up on bugs until they are fixed;
  • work with developers, designers and product owners to catch problems early.
